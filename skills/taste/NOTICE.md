# Provenance Notice

This skill is an independently rewritten adaptation of ideas from:

- `Leonxlnx/taste-skill` at `ccbc15639c97057cbfcf32ecebc38ef716e4bb37`, MIT License. Consulted `skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md` (the "tasteskill: Anti-Slop Frontend Skill" v2) for brief inference and the design read, the three dials, the design-system selection map, color, shape, and theme consistency locks, typography and spacing discipline, motion motivation and reduced-motion rules, the dark-mode parity protocol, the audit-first redesign protocol with preservation rules and modernisation levers, the banned-pattern list, and the pre-flight checklist. Reuse type: adapted.

No source file was copied wholesale. The skill rewrites the material in this repository's concise voice, removes stack mandates so it stays stack-neutral, drops the upstream "not for dashboards" scoping in favor of an explicit split between landing-page rules and rules that also apply to product UI, and positions the result as a rules layer that co-invokes with `frontend-design` and defers flow and structure to `ux-design`.
